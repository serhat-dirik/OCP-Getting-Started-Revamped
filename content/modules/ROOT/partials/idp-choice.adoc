*Signing in to a platform tool? Choose `workshop-users`.*

This cluster has *two* identity providers wired at once, so OpenShift's sign-in page offers a choice.
They appear in this order, and *the first one is not the one you want*:

* *`rhbk`* — the Red Hat build of Keycloak, listed *first*. It secures the *sample application* later in the workshop and knows nothing about your workshop account.
* *`workshop-users`* — *the one you want.* Sign in with your workshop username (`{user}`) and password.

*If you picked `rhbk` by mistake, it does not look like a mistake.* You get a normal-looking username and password form (titled *Sign in to sso*), and your workshop credentials are simply rejected — there is no error explaining why. If a sign-in you expected to work keeps refusing correct credentials, go back to the chooser and pick *`workshop-users`*.

*This applies to OpenShift's own sign-in page only.* Some tools have their own front door that never touches OpenShift OAuth, and a Guest/GitHub choice there is normal and correct — *Developer Hub* opens on exactly such a page (*"Select a sign-in method: Guest / GitHub"*), where *Guest* is the intended one click, and *Gitea* asks for a username and password in its own form. Neither offers `workshop-users`, and neither is a wrong turn.
